Moving to QGIS

You are now leaving Planscape to open QGIS. To upload your data:

  1. Click the ‘Continue to QGIS’ button to open QGIS.
  2. In QGIS, select Plugins from the top menu bar and choose ‘Planscape Plugin’.
  3. Log in to QGIS using your Planscape email and password.
  4. Workspace selection
  5. Upload and format your data within QGIS.
  6. Return to Planscape and check the Uploaded Data Layers section on the map viewer to see your new layers.
