What are Advanced Stand-Level Constraints?

Stand-level constraints define conditions a stand must meet for ForSys to consider it for treatment. Select an available data layer, choose an operator, and enter a value.

For continuous data, set a numeric condition, such as Probability of High Severity Fire > 0.

For categorical data, enter the numeric category code. For example, if Owl Habitat uses 1 = habitat and 0 = non-habitat, use Owl Habitat = 0 to exclude habitat. Check the layer’s information icon and source documentation to confirm what the codes represent.

Advanced constraints apply when the scenario runs and do not update the setup map or treatable percentage. Your scenario may fail if too few eligible stands remain to meet your treatment targets.
